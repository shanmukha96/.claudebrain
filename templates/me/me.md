# Memory 2a: how this user works

Knowledge ABOUT the person you work with - not rules you follow. Ground truth you reason WITH.
One dated line per observation, and merge with an existing line rather than adding a near-duplicate.

Worth recording: how they want to be told things, what they have corrected you on, a preference
stated outright, the way they reason about a problem, and what they have deprioritised so you
stop raising it.

NOT worth recording: your own failures - those go in mistakes.md - or anything you inferred from
one incident and stated as a general pattern. Note the evidence base of an entry when it is thin.
