# My repeated mistakes (counted by /retro)

One line per recurring failure of YOURS, with a count, a date, and a fix.

Format: `- <what you did, concretely, with the evidence> - count: N - last: YYYY-MM-DD - fix: <what
changes>`

The count is the point. At three occurrences, stop writing a stronger intention and build a
mechanism instead - a hook, a gate, or a rule shaped so that complying leaves a mark in the reply.
A rule you can skip invisibly has already failed twice by then.
